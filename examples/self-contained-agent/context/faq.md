## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an appointment?**
No. Walk in during opening hours. Home and office visits need an
appointment: call 555-0142.

**Which devices do you repair?**
Windows and Linux laptops and desktops, MacBooks and iMacs, Android phones,
iPhones, and tablets. We do not repair game consoles, printers, or TVs.

**How long does a repair take?**
See "Usual time" in the services table. We phone you when the device is
ready.

**Is my data safe?**
We do not open your files unless the repair needs it, and we ask first.
Back up your data before you bring the device in, if you can. We are not
responsible for data loss on a drive that is already failing.

**Do you give a warranty?**
Yes. Each repair has a 90-day warranty on parts and labour. Liquid damage
repairs have a 30-day warranty.

**Which payments do you accept?**
Cash, debit and credit cards, and contactless payment. Payment is on
collection. We do not take cheques.

**What if the device cannot be repaired?**
You pay only the diagnostic check (30). We can recycle the device for free,
and we wipe the storage first.

**How long do you keep a device that is not collected?**
60 days after we tell you it is ready. After that, we may recycle it.

**Do you sell used computers?**
Yes, a small number of refurbished laptops, each with a 6-month warranty.
Ask in the shop for the current stock.

**Can I check the status of my repair?**
Yes. Phone 555-0142 or email help@riverside-repair.example with the job
number from your receipt.
